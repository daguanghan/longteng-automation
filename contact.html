Frequently asked questions

How long does a machine or line take?

Single machines typically take several weeks from design approval; complete lines take longer. We confirm the schedule in the quotation.

Can you install and commission in Vietnam?

Yes. Our technicians travel from Guangzhou (about 2 hours' flight to Hanoi) to install, commission and train operators at your factory.

Can we see the machine before shipment?

Yes. Every machine passes a factory acceptance test (FAT) in Guangzhou, which you can attend on site or by video.

What about import duties?

Most assembly, test and inspection machines are imported into Vietnam at 0% MFN duty; conveyors can benefit from the ASEAN–China certificate of origin (Form E). We prepare the export documents; please confirm duties with your customs broker.

How do you support us after installation?

Remote diagnosis, a recommended spare-parts kit with each machine, and a named contact for any question.

Have a product that needs automating?

Send us a photo, drawing or video of your product and process. We reply within one working day.
